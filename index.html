Why Standard Accuracy is Fatal in Fraud Detection

A naive model predicting every transaction as Legitimate would achieve an impressive 99.827% Accuracy, yet miss 100% of frauds, causing massive financial chargeback damages. That is why our pipeline optimizes for Precision-Recall AUC (PR-AUC), Sensitivity (Recall), and asymmetric financial loss functions.
